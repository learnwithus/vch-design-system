# Graphics and imagery

## Wave pattern ([guide](../sources/brandfolder/08-design-elements.md))
- The wave pattern is inspired by the region's coastlines and topography. Apply it to **layout edges** or **inside solid shapes**.
- It is optional. Use it only when the layout has room and benefits from it.
- **Allowed backgrounds only:** Ocean Blue, Harbour Teal, Seaside Teal, Rainforest Green, Sea Star Purple.
- Pattern files are **pre-tinted to 80%** for use on a 100% background of the same colour. **Never recolour them.**
- Never place **text** or **images** over the pattern. Give it clear space.
- The master `.ai` files (RGB and CMYK) are on Brandfolder, or ask brand@vch.ca.

## Photography ([guide](../sources/brandfolder/07-photography.md))
- **Photography first**, ahead of illustration or clip art.
- Show real people connecting naturally: staff together, staff with patients. Avoid staged shots.
- Images should be bright, naturally lit, warm or neutral, with colours that sit with the palette. **No tints, filters or distortion.**
- Use shallow depth of field to focus on the human connection or a device.
- **Show diversity and counter stereotypes**, for example male nurses and female physicians.
- **AI-generated images:** not for clinical, instructional or trust-critical content. Use them for conceptual or decorative purposes only.
- **Portraits:** natural setting, uncluttered background.
- **Action shots:** unexpected angles, authentic moments, few people in frame.
- **Nature:** calm, bright, varied coastal landscapes. No traffic or stormy water.
- **Facilities:** exterior, eye level, showing the main entrance. Shoot in daylight with blue sky, or at sunset with a purple glow. No emergency vehicles.

## Illustration
Use illustration only when photography isn't possible, or to soften difficult topics. Recolour it to the VCH palette, keep it realistic rather than cartoonish, and include diverse body types.

## Icons
- Keep one consistent icon style per property. The guide shows three acceptable styles as images.
- The values, goals and pillars icon set is **reserved** for those topics.
- Signage has its own standardised icon and arrow set (download from Brandfolder).
