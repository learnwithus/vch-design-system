# Voice and messaging

## Core statements ([intro](../sources/brandfolder/00-introduction.md))
- **Vision:** *Healthy lives in healthy communities.* This is also the vch.ca homepage title.
- **Purpose:** to deliver an exceptional care experience for all.
- **Values:**
  - **We Care for Everyone:** for patients, families, colleagues and ourselves.
  - **We are Always Learning:** curious, and open to innovation.
  - **We Strive for Better Results:** better patient outcomes, across all functions.
- **Pillars:** Indigenous Cultural Safety · Equity, Diversity and Inclusion · Anti-Racism · Planetary Health.
- **Strategic goals:** Exceptional Care · Research and Innovation for Impact · Great Place to Work · Healthy People.

## Writing ([guide](../sources/brandfolder/04-our-written-brand.md))
- The **VCH Editorial Style Guide** (PDF on Brandfolder, not captured here) governs terminology, grammar and capitalisation.
- It applies to promotional and marketing material, media and social, oneVCH news, memos, and submissions to governing bodies.
- It does not apply to technical, scholarly or research writing. Social media allows some flexibility.
- Spelling is Canadian English: *colour*, *centre*.
- Exceptions go to your Communications lead or brand@vch.ca.

## Tone, as observed on vch.ca
The site's tone is plain-language, service-first and warm. It opens with a land acknowledgement in the footer. "oneVCH" is the internal staff brand.
