# Print, signage and templates

## Signage colour by facility type ([guide](../sources/brandfolder/09-signage-guidelines.md))
| Facility type | Colour |
|---|---|
| VCH primary brand, Hospitals, Rural Health Care Centres, Health Units, MHSU services | **Ocean Blue + Cloud White** |
| Urgent and Primary Care Centres (UPCC) | **Sea Star Purple** |
| Community Health Centres | **Rainforest Green** |
| Family Health Clinics | **Harbour Teal** |
| Multiple facilities on one sign | Default to Ocean Blue + white |

Preferred: a facility-colour background with the white VCH logo. Alternative: the full-colour logo on a white panel, with the facility name in white on a facility-colour panel. Interior wayfinding guidance is listed as "coming soon".

## Templates ([guide](../sources/brandfolder/06-templates.md))
All templates are downloaded from Brandfolder, and printed items are ordered through PHSA printing services.

- **VCH (external):** letterhead, memo, PowerPoint (standard, Indigenous art, PHC, PHC+PHSA), fax cover, email signature, Word posters (letter and tabloid, blue or white, with or without image), brochure, report.
- **oneVCH (internal only):** memo, PowerPoint, Board/SET report and deck, SET briefing note, posters (blue, white or **teal**), factsheet.

Print uses CMYK or Pantone values from [01-colour](01-colour.md). Sunset Orange is intended for print.
