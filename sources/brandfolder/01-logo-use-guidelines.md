# Logo use guidelines


## Logo

### VCH logo

Vancouver Coastal Health's logo is reflective of the communities who we serve - from the coast to the mountains.  In 2019, the VCH logo was given a minor refresh to remove our outdated tagline and to add consistency in our font. Please ensure you are using our most recent version of the logo.

This logo should be used on most materials produced by people at Vancouver Coastal Health. If you are a part of Program, Centre or Clinic, you can also consider using the Program and Facility logo system to feature the name of your entity to help patients, residents, and families find you. 

VCH has one horixontal logo, there are no stacked or vertical versions available. 

You can download various file formats and versions of the logo in the brand collection folder.

## Logo usage

### Logo usage

Clear space is the required amount of space around an object that is clear of any other logos, text, symbols or artwork. Maintaining the adequate clear space around a logo ensures the logo can be seen quickly and prevents it from being crowded or overlapped by other content. The clear space ( X ) around the logo is equal to the height of the "C" in Coastal. The same clear space is applied to all versions of our logo.

## Graphic mountain symbol

### Graphic mountain symbol

The graphic symbol consists of a mountain and wave to represent the VCH regions. At nearly all times, the graphic symbol should only be used within the complete VCH logo, as shown above.

The graphic symbol may not be used alone to represent VCH externally as it as not recognizable without our name. In special circumstances, it may be used on its own (e.g. internally between health authorities, or as social media profile pictures) as long as Vancouver Coastal Health is written on the same page, slide or similar component. Please consult with the brand team if you wish to use the mountain symbol on its own via brand@vch.ca.

## Using logo with partner logos

Using logo with partners

Always maintain adequate distance between logos when displaying our VCH logo with partner logos. Do not add divider lines between the other logos.

When logos are differently shaped from one another, aim to display them with equal visual weight.

You may place the logos on any corner of a layout, as long as they are grouped together in one line, as shown in this example below.

## Using the logo on a background

Using the logo on a background

Follow these guidelines to know which backgrounds can pair with our logo.

To use our logo on photography, make sure the logo is easy to read. You may use the full-colour logo on a photo background if the area is bright white or white with slight tonal variations. The entire logo should be visible and readable with proper space around it. If the photgraphy doesn't have a bright white space or solid colour to use the black or white logo, try adding in a bar of colour on the bottom or top of the photo to place the logo in.
