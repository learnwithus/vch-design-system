# Digital branding guidelines


## Brand basics

### Digital Branding Guidelines

When you brand any externally-facing digital product, whether it’s a website, web application or mobile app, with the Vancouver Coastal Health brand, it must follow the VCH’s branding guidelines in accordance with our Communications, Branding and Media policy . Any product that is available outside of the VCH network is considered externally facing. 

In addition to following the brand guidelines, digital products must also comply with all applicable VCH policies, standards and procedures.  The guidelines are intended for use by internal VCH teams, PDHIS teams and for external vendors collaborating on VCH digital products. 

Consistent application of these standards helps support patient recognition, quality and trust in the VCH brand.  

This brand guide is designed to help your product also align with digital accessibility standards. Websites and applications at Vancouver Coastal Health should meet the global standard accessibility rating of WCAG 2.2 AA.

If you are still in the planning stages but have not yet developed your product with a team or vendor, there are teams at VCH that can assist with both content, design and technology.

### Brand basics

Colours 

Colours follow the VCH brand colours. With digital use, ensure you use adequate colour contrast. To test colour contrast, use the Web Aim tool for testing or reach out to the Digital Communications Team for support if you are unsure.  

Photography, illustrations and icons  

Photography, illustrations and guidelines follows the VCH brand for photography, illustrations and icons.  

If you are unable to find an existing asset to use for your digital product, email brand@vch.ca and we can help you find or create an asset that aligns with brand to meet your needs.  

Writing  

Writing in digital products follows the VCH written brand.  

Logo and Favicon

Please note, in accordance with our Branding policy, unique non-VCH branded logos for applications, teams and programs are not permitted. Logos must follow the VCH logo use guidelines.

Alert messages An alert banner displays an emergency site-wide alert.   

A notification bar displays an important site-wide notice of a service or process change (eg, upcoming outages or slowness, etc.). 

Pop-up alerts should be used sparingly.
Forms

Digital forms on any VCH product must align with all relevant guidelines and policies to ensure privacy and security for clients and Vancouver Coastal Health. Please ensure privacy and security teams are involved in any form development to ensure this aligns.  

If you are working with a platform that does not allow you to change form styles, ensure you are using VCH font and style colours at minimum and that form styles and functionality comply with accessibility standards WCAG 2.2 AA.
Accordion 

Accordions display content inside a container that the user can open and close. Use accordions sparingly to reduce effort for the user and search engines in reading content. Accordions are useful for long lists of FAQs, step by step content, or long list of content where a user may only access one piece of information.
