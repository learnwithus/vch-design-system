# Program and facility logos


## Guidelines for program and facility logos

### Guidelines for program and facility logos

In addition to the Vancouver Coastal Health logo, some facilities and programs may qualify for a logo lockup as seen below.

This lockup has been created  as a way to help patients and residents find and use our front-line services while, at the same time, ensuring that they understand that Vancouver Coastal Health has the overall responsibility for their care. 

This logo lockup is not available for teams or departments. 

It is against VCH policy to create a logo without approval from the VCH Communitions, Equity and Partnerships team (formally Communications and Public Affairs). Any logos previously created should no longer be in use. 

If you require a department or program logo lockup, please contact brand@vch.ca
When should I use the program and facility logo?

In most circumstances, the VCH logo is all that is required. However, if you communicate directly with patients, residents, caregivers and familites, it may be helpful to use the facility or program logo lockup. If your service is not patient-facing, please use the VCH logo.

The facility and program logo lockup is useful for the following applications:

- Site signage
- Patient-facing information such as posters, pre-post surgey instructions, care manuals - anything that might help the patient identify your hospital, clinic or program.
Who is eligible for a progam or facility logo?

Most Vancouver Coastal Health entities can use the VCH Logo for most needs. To avoid the proliferation of unnecessary logos, we will restrict these custom program and facility Logo Lockups to clinics, programs and centres that communicate directly with patients, families and residents. Think of it as a wayfinding tool so they can easily find you and communicate with you. If this isn't a consideration, you should use the VCH Brand or site name/logo where you are based.
